Mark Dyson.

Professional Photographer

Welcome to my personal profile. I’m glad you’re here.

I’m a portrait photographerbased in Rotterdam, working from my daylight studio and on location. My approach to portrait photography is natural, relaxed, and personal. Every session begins with a conversation, creating space to connect, feel comfortable, and let your personality come through naturally. Rather than relying on fixed poses or forced expressions, I use simple movements and gentle guidance to capture genuine moments and authentic character.

I work with artists, founders, creatives, and independent teams who want portraits that feel expressive, honest, and true to who they are. Whether the goal is to tell a personal story, present a professional identity, or create a strong visual presence for a brand, I aim to make images that go beyond appearance and reveal something meaningful about the person in front of the camera.

For me, portrait photography is about more than taking a picture. It’s about creating an atmosphere where people can be themselves and capturing the individuality, confidence, and subtle details that make each person unique.

Thank you for being here. I invite you to explore my work and get in touch if you’d like to create something together.
